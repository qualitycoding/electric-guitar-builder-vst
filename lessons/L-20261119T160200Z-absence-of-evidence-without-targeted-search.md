## Trigger
Research concludes that no evidence exists for an effect (here: neck-joint construction on sound) from the sources already open, without a search aimed at that question.
## What went wrong
C-028 stated 'no measured support for a neck-joint effect', based on Zollner's journalism survey. When the owner asked directly, one targeted search found a controlled conversion experiment (Mottola 2007), a nine-guitar study (Paté et al.) and a JASA paper on the mechanism.
## Correction
C-028 superseded by C-034 (measured but inconsistent effects); C-035 added; source register, D-023, R-011 and the assumption audit updated.
## Prevention rule
Before recording an absence-of-evidence claim, run at least two searches phrased around the specific comparison (for example 'X vs Y measurement study'), list them in the round log, and word the claim as 'none found in the searches listed'.
## Detection check
Every claim containing 'no evidence', 'no measured support' or 'none found' cites at least two targeted search queries in its round log.
