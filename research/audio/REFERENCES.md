# Reference audio register

The authoritative machine-readable register is `REFERENCES.json`. All references are **Guitar-TECHS v1**, Zenodo record **14963133**, licensed **CC BY 4.0**. Use only the **direct-input** tracks for candidate/reference comparisons; amplified and room recordings are contextual references, not blind-pair sources.

- Dataset and download files: https://zenodo.org/records/14963133
- Project methodology and hardware: https://guitar-techs.github.io/
- Paper: Pedroza, Abreu, Corey and Roman, ‘Guitar-TECHS: An Electric Guitar Dataset Covering Techniques, Musical Excerpts, Chords and Scales Using a Diverse Array of Hardware’, ICASSP 2025. https://arxiv.org/html/2501.03720v1
- Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Retain this attribution in every evidence bundle: ‘Guitar-TECHS by Hegel Pedroza, Wallace Abreu, Ryan M. Corey and Iran R. Roman, Zenodo record 14963133, CC BY 4.0; clips windowed, loudness-matched and faded for evaluation; no endorsement implied.’

The register covers normal notes, palm mutes, scales, chords and musical excerpts across bridge, neck and bridge–neck pickup configurations and 0.009–0.042, 0.010–0.046 and 0.011–0.050 string sets. It does **not** independently vary every construction parameter. Therefore parameter-effect tests use analytic/model-oracle checks and listening demos; they must not claim dataset-proven isolated material or fret-size fidelity.
