# Amendment 03 — sources checked after R7 (owner question, 2026-11-19)

The owner asked whether any evidence shows set-neck or bolt-on construction sounding different from neck-through. A targeted search found measurements that round 2 had missed; C-028 ('no measured support') was too strong and is superseded by C-034. C-035 corroborates the D-022 structural model and the D-026 polarisation weighting.
