Guitar-TECHS v1 (Zenodo 14963133, CC BY 4.0) provides DI and contextual amplified/room tracks. For synthesis validation, reserve P2 from fitting, use P1 for calibration, and preserve onset in listening clips. Dataset variation does not independently establish every construction parameter's real-world fidelity.
